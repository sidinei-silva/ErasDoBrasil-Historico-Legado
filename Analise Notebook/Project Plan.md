# Project Plan: Eras do Brasil

## 1.0 Vision and Strategic Overview

This document outlines the foundational vision for 'Eras do Brasil', detailing the project's high-level concept, core mission, and strategic market entry. It serves as a guiding star for the development team, stakeholders, and potential investors, articulating the strategic framework designed to deliver a unique and commercially successful role-playing experience. 'Eras do Brasil' is a historical-fantasy Role-Playing Game (RPG) set in a mystical version of Brazil, where players navigate and influence different pivotal eras of the nation's past.

Our core mission is to create an immersive RPG experience that masterfully intertwines Brazilian history and folklore with innovative and strategically rewarding gameplay. We aim to build a world that is not only compelling but also culturally significant, offering players a unique window into a setting rarely explored in the global RPG landscape.

To achieve this, the project will follow a strategic two-pronged development approach. This begins with a tabletop RPG MVP, a critical, low-cost strategy to de-risk our primary investment. It allows us to rigorously test, balance, and validate our core gameplay loop and build a foundational community before committing significant capital to digital development. Following this validation, we will execute a phased digital adaptation, translating the proven systems into a persistent and evolving online world.

This strategic overview establishes the 'why' behind our endeavor. The following sections will break down the 'what' and the 'how', providing a detailed project scope and a clear roadmap for development.

## 2.0 Product Definition and Scope

Clearly defining the product's scope is critical to managing expectations, guiding development priorities, and ensuring a focused and successful execution. This section provides a detailed breakdown of both the initial tabletop RPG and the long-term digital adaptation, specifying what will be built in each phase and outlining the key features that will differentiate 'Eras do Brasil' in a competitive market.

### 2.1. The Tabletop RPG: Core System Validation (MVP)

The tabletop RPG will serve as the project's Minimum Viable Product (MVP). Its primary function is to provide a robust platform for testing, balancing, and validating the core game mechanics and world-building concepts before committing to significant digital development investment. This initial release will allow us to gather direct player feedback, build a dedicated community, and refine the gameplay loop into a polished and compelling experience.

The fundamental system mechanics for the tabletop MVP are as follows:

- **System Core:** Action resolution will be based on a D20 system, with outcomes determined by a roll plus relevant attribute and proficiency modifiers against a set Difficulty Class (CD).
- **Character Pillars:** Players will create characters from one of three distinct Origins: **Colonizador**, **Indígena**, or **Ser Folclórico**, each offering unique narrative hooks and mechanical starting points.
- **Class System:** The game will feature a flexible class structure with 12 initial classes (four per Origin). Classes evolve through three Tiers of specialization, allowing for deep progression within a single era.
- **Progression:** A unique character progression model allows players to switch freely between unlocked classes. Upon unlocking a new class, its active skills are **permanently added** to the character's global skill pool, while **passive skills are NOT inherited**. This is the core of creating "Builds Híbridas," enabling an unparalleled depth of strategic hybrid builds.

### 2.2. The Digital Adaptation: Long-Term Vision

The ultimate vision is for the digital adaptation of 'Eras do Brasil' to become a persistent-world RPG, where player actions have lasting consequences and the world evolves over time. The development will be approached in two strategic phases to manage complexity and ensure a solid foundation.

1. **Phase 1 (Prototype):** This initial digital version will feature a scene-based world. Players will navigate between distinct locations or "blocks" via on-screen indicators. The combat system will be static and turn-based, focusing on action selection rather than character positioning on a grid.
2. **Phase 2 (Full Release):** The prototype will evolve into the full game vision. World navigation will shift to free point-and-click movement within a turn-based time system (like _Stoneshard_), where time passes in ticks with each action. The combat system will be upgraded to a tactical, grid-based model, offering deeper strategic gameplay inspired by titles like _Wartales_ and _Solasta_.

## 3.0 Target Audience and Market Positioning

A deep and nuanced understanding of our target player is essential for every aspect of development, from core game design and narrative tone to marketing strategy and long-term commercial success. This section profiles our primary and secondary audiences and articulates the project's precise positioning within the current market landscape.

### 3.1. Primary and Secondary Audience Profiles

- **Primary Audience:** Our primary target consists of experienced RPG players who are deeply engaged with both tabletop and digital formats. This group includes enthusiasts of D20-based tabletop systems and players of tactical, persistent-world digital RPGs. They are drawn to games with deep mechanical systems, strategic combat, and meaningful character progression, with inspirations drawn from titles like _Orna_, _Albion_, _Solasta_, _New World_, and _Ravendawn_.
- **Secondary Audience:** Our secondary audience is broader, encompassing players with a strong interest in historical-fantasy settings, world culture, and unique, narrative-driven experiences. This includes individuals fascinated by Brazilian history and folklore, as well as players who are actively seeking games that offer fresh perspectives and settings beyond traditional fantasy tropes.

### 3.2. Market Positioning

The contemporary RPG market is crowded but also highly segmented. 'Eras do Brasil' is strategically positioned to fill a significant gap by offering a high-quality, mechanically deep RPG experience rooted in a novel and culturally rich setting. While many games draw from European, North American, or East Asian mythologies, South American folklore and history remain a vast, underexplored territory.

The project's unique blend of a D20 foundation, a highly flexible class-switching system, and a narrative that spans multiple historical eras creates a compelling and currently underserved niche. By delivering a polished and immersive experience, 'Eras do Brasil' will establish itself not just as another RPG, but as a landmark title that introduces a new and exciting world to the global gaming community.

## 4.0 Core Gameplay and Mechanics

This section details the interlocking systems engineered to create our target high-retention engagement loop. These mechanics are designed to be intuitive for seasoned RPG players while offering unique twists that provide strategic depth and high replayability.

### 4.1. Character Progression and Engagement Loop

To ensure deep, long-term progression and avoid content bottlenecks, our system features a multi-layered evolution.

- **Class Tiers:** Each of the 12 initial classes can be evolved through three distinct **Tiers** (Tier 1-3). The Class Tier system is a deliberate design choice to provide vertical progression _within_ an era, ensuring a rich and rewarding mid-game without making players feel progression is locked behind new era releases.
- **The "Moeda de Classe":** Unlocking a new class or advancing to a higher tier requires a rare, in-game currency known as the **"Moeda de Classe"**. This currency is obtained by finding specific mentor NPCs and completing challenging quests, creating a core gameplay loop of exploration, overcoming challenges, and being rewarded with major character advancement opportunities.
- **Strategic Class Switching:** Upon unlocking a new class, its active skills are **permanently added** to the character's global skill pool. This allows players to mix and match active skills from any class they have ever unlocked, enabling an unparalleled depth of strategic hybrid builds and incentivizing experimentation.

### 4.2. Combat and Action Resolution

The core of all action resolution is a D20-based system designed for clarity and tactical decision-making.

- **Core Resolution:** Actions are resolved by rolling **1D20 + Attribute Modifier + applicable proficiency or skill bonuses** against a target's Difficulty Class (CD). This familiar framework provides a solid foundation.
- **Combat Evolution:** The combat system is designed to evolve alongside the digital adaptation from a static, turn-based model in the prototype to a tactical, grid-based system in the full release, offering a much deeper layer of strategic gameplay.

### 4.3. Economy and Crafting

A robust economy and crafting system will empower players to create their own gear and influence the world.

- **Lifeskills System:** Non-combat skills are categorized into two main branches: **Coleta (Gathering)** and **Produção (Crafting)**, allowing players to develop specialized roles.
- **Itemization Framework:** The power and properties of all equipment are determined by a **5x5 matrix of Quality and Rarity**. This system dictates an item's stats, properties, and required level. Strategically, this creates a long-tail content strategy and a powerful loot-chasing incentive loop, ensuring "replay value with the same weapon in different versions" and making both crafting and looting highly rewarding pursuits.

### 4.4. Narrative and World Interaction

The game's narrative is woven directly into its core mechanics, providing a rich context for the player's journey.

- **Core Lore:** The overarching narrative is driven by two key concepts. The **"Raiz do Mundo" (World Root)** is the mystical force that connects all eras of Brazil. Characters are "awoken" to this connection through the **"Dom da Revivência" (Gift of Revival)**, which provides the in-game justification for their ability to traverse different time periods.
- **Content Structure:** The game's content will be delivered through an introductory **Prologue** where players first experience the "Dom da Revivência," a main story campaign (**Act 1**), origin-specific **Mini-Campaigns**, and an **"Eco-Histórica"** timeline that reimagines real history as a source of adventure.

These mechanics form the bedrock of the 'Eras do Brasil' experience, providing a clear path for the phased development detailed in the roadmap below.

## 5.0 Development Roadmap

This roadmap outlines a strategic, phased development plan designed to mitigate risk, validate core concepts, and build community momentum over time. Each phase has clear objectives and deliverables, ensuring a structured and transparent path from the initial tabletop concept to the full digital release.

### 5.1. Phase I: Tabletop RPG Development and Validation

- **Objective:** To finalize, test, and validate the core rule system of 'Eras do Brasil' while building an initial community of engaged players and playtesters. This phase is critical for gathering feedback and ensuring the fundamental gameplay is balanced and enjoyable.
- **Key Deliverables:**
    - A complete Core Rulebook document.
    - A printable, professionally designed Character Sheet.
    - A set of initial playtest scenarios.

### 5.2. Phase II: Digital Prototype (Block-Based World)

- **Objective:** The objective is to create a playable vertical slice that proves the technical feasibility of our core digital systems and validates the player engagement loop in a digital format, serving as a key milestone for securing Phase III funding.
- **Key Deliverables:**
    - A playable digital build featuring block-to-block world navigation.
    - Implementation of the static, turn-based combat system.
    - 1-2 fully playable classes.
    - One complete, explorable region.
- **Proposed Technology Stack:**
    - **Frontend:** React + TypeScript
    - **Backend:** FastAPI
    - **Database:** AWS DocumentDB or PostgreSQL

### 5.3. Phase III: Expanded Digital Game (Full Vision)

- **Objective:** To evolve the digital prototype into a feature-complete game that fully realizes the project's long-term vision of a persistent, living world with deep tactical gameplay.
- **Key Features for Implementation:**
    - Transition to free point-and-click world movement (turn-based).
    - Implementation of the tactical, grid-based combat system.
    - Integration of the full roster of classes, items, and crafting recipes.
    - Development of a persistent world with evolving NPCs and dynamic events.

This phased roadmap provides a clear developmental path. The final section explores advanced systems that will further enrich the final digital product.

## 6.0 Advanced Digital Systems and Post-Launch Vision

This section details advanced, optional systems designed to create a deeply engaging, high-stakes, and dynamic world in the final digital version of 'Eras do Brasil'. These mechanics aim to foster emergent narratives and provide long-term replayability beyond the core content.

### 6.1. Emergent Narrative Systems

The following mechanics are planned for the full digital release to foster emergent, personal narratives and increase player investment.

- **Item Durability & Repair:** Equipment will degrade with use, requiring maintenance via crafting lifeskills. Should an item break, it is not permanently lost but converts to valuable 'scrap' or 'fragments,' which can be used in repair or new crafting recipes, adding a meaningful resource management loop.
- **Full Loot on Death:** An optional hardcore mode where players drop their equipped items upon defeat. This high-risk, high-reward system creates genuine tension and makes every encounter meaningful, turning simple gear recovery into an emergent quest.
- **Evolving Enemies:** Enemies that successfully defeat a player will gain experience and strength. This system creates powerful, personal narratives by allowing antagonists to grow alongside the player. For example, "The thief who robbed me returned weeks later as the leader of an ambush clan," transforming a random encounter into a recurring rival with a shared history.

### 6.2. Post-Launch Vision

The full release of the digital game is not the end of the journey but the beginning of an ever-evolving world. The long-term vision for 'Eras do Brasil' includes the potential for introducing multiplayer features, allowing players to adventure together. Furthermore, we plan to deliver a continuous stream of new content, including additional "Eras" to explore, new classes to master, and new story acts that expand upon the rich lore of this unique historical-fantasy universe.
