## Claude:

Escolhi o **Lobo Lendário** — classe Tier 1 da Origem Folclórica do teu GDD. É a fusão entre espírito e fera: um predador ágil e instintivo, moldado pelas lendas populares, com garras integradas ao corpo e conexão sobrenatural com o mundo espiritual.

Baseei o prompt na lore da classe (garras de ossos ancestrais, manto de peles totêmicas, pingente de dente encantado) e na direção de arte do jogo (paleta terrosa, mística, Brasil colonial de 1497).

```
Full-body 3D character model of "Lobo Lendário" (Legendary Wolf), a folkloric spirit-beast warrior from a mystical colonial-Brazil (1497) fantasy setting.

CHARACTER: A lean, athletic humanoid-wolf hybrid — human-like posture and proportions but with lupine features: a wolf-shaped head with alert pointed ears, glowing amber eyes, a short muzzle with visible fangs, digitigrade wolf-like legs, and a bushy tail. Grayish-brown fur with darker markings along the spine and shoulders. Build is agile and predatory, not bulky — built for speed and stealth, not brute strength.

WEAPONS/ANATOMY: Bone-white ancestral claws fused directly into both forearms and hands (not held weapons — organic, integrated, slightly runic engravings glowing faint pale-blue along the claw edges).

CLOTHING/ARMOR: A tattered mantle made of stitched totemic animal pelts draped over one shoulder and the back, secondary option of a woven cloak of spectral thorny fibers with a faint spiritual shimmer. Minimal leather straps and cord wrappings around torso, waist, and forearms — rustic, handcrafted, tribal aesthetic, not metallic or industrial.

ACCESSORIES: An enchanted ancestral tooth pendant hanging from a leather cord around the neck, faintly glowing with moonlit-blue light. Small bone/feather talismans tied into the fur or straps.

COLOR PALETTE: Earthy browns and grays for fur, aged gold and bone-white for claws and teeth, deep mystical blue-purple accents for magical glow effects, muted forest green undertones in the cloak fabric.

STYLE: Stylized fantasy game character, hand-painted PBR textures, semi-realistic proportions (not cartoonish, not photorealistic) — suitable for a rustic eco-fantasy RPG with Brazilian folklore and indigenous/colonial influences. Grounded, earthy, spiritual atmosphere rather than sleek or futuristic.

POSE: Dynamic mid-lunge stalking pose, low crouch, weight forward, one clawed hand extended, tail low and alert — conveying agility, ambush-predator instinct, and readiness to strike.

TECHNICAL: Clean topology, game-ready low-to-mid poly mesh, T-pose or A-pose variant available for rigging, PBR material maps (albedo, normal, roughness, metallic), neutral studio lighting with soft rim light to emphasize silhouette, plain neutral background.
```
